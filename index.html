Every gram earns its place.

Pull an SP-1 apart and you find three layers: a knit upper, a thin foam heel and a carbon spike plate. Nothing else made the cut.

  • 3 layers
  • 4 mm drop

Spike plate

Seven pins. Zero slip.

Seven 7 mm pyramid pins sit under the ball of your foot, where you push hardest out of the blocks. The plate is stiff enough to hand that push back.

  • 7 pins
  • 7 mm each
  • 1.2 mm plate
